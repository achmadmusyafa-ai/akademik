## Brand & Style

### Personality & Tone
The design system embodies the calm, trustworthy, and disciplined essence of a modern Islamic primary education institution. It blends institutional reliability with warm, nurturing clarity. The tone is respectful, structured, and tranquil, avoiding sterile enterprise coldness while maintaining high operational efficiency for teachers, administrators, and parents.

### Target Audience
- Primary school administrative staff managing student master data and admissions.
- Homeroom and subject teachers inputting Tahfidz progress, daily assessments, and report cards.
- School principals and foundation leadership reviewing academic health metrics and financial summaries.

### Emotional Response
Users should feel clarity, tranquility, and competence. Daily academic administrative duties (grading, Tahfidz memorization tracking, attendance) should feel effortless and organized rather than bureaucratic.

### Visual Style
**Modern Islamic Corporate Clean**: 
- Crisp structure built upon purposeful whitespace and refreshing botanical teal foundations.
- Gentle surfaces and tonal containment rather than heavy dark shadows.
- Micro-interactions designed with calm fluidity to reduce cognitive strain during peak grading cycles.
- Subtle geometric rhythm in cards and sidebars reminiscent of disciplined, organized scholastic ledgers.

## Colors

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#287A74` - Deep Pine Teal)**: Serves as the core institutional anchor. Used for primary navigation states, structural headers, high-priority actions, and brand identity elements.
- **Secondary (`#55A9A0` - Soft Jade Teal)**: Used for supporting interactive elements, active tabs, secondary buttons, chart series, and contextual progress bars.
- **Tertiary / Accent Highlight (`#AEEED3` - Mint Foam)**: A luminous, refreshing accent used for success indicators, active badge backings, Tahfidz milestone completions, and high-visibility soft highlights.
- **Neutral Accent / Warm Gold (`#FFF8B0` - Pastel Pale Cream)**: Reserved for alert banners, pending payment badges, memorization review markers, and warm contextual warnings without triggering panic.

### Surface & Functional Extensions
- **Canvas Base**: `#F7FAF9` (Off-white with a whisper of mint coolness, eliminating clinical glare).
- **Surface Elevation 1**: `#FFFFFF` (Pristine card and table containers).
- **Surface Elevation 2**: `#EFF7F5` (Sidebar background, nested modules, and grouped table headers).
- **Text Primary**: `#163330` (Ultra-deep teal-black for maximum contrast and legibility).
- **Text Muted**: `#5C7A76` (Balanced mid-tone for secondary labels and timestamps).
- **Border Subtle**: `#E0ECE9` (Delicate structural dividers).
- **Critical / Danger**: `#D9534F` (Reserved exclusively for disciplinary alerts or system errors).

## Typography

### Typographic Rationale
**Plus Jakarta Sans** is utilized across all tiers. Its open counters, clean geometric rhythm, and subtle rounded terminals convey modern professionalism while remaining approachable for everyday educational use.

### Usage Rules
- **Display & Headlines**: Styled in weights `600` and `700` with slight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to ground dashboard statistics, academic term overviews, and section leads.
- **Numerals & Metrics**: Grade tables, student NISN IDs, and Tahfidz surah counters require clear tabular rendering. Tabular figure styling (`font-feature-settings: "tnum"`) must be enabled on all data tables.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Labels across badges, status chips, and table headers adopt uppercase or title-case formatting with tight letter spacing for rapid scanning across dense academic rosters.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
- **Sidebar-Centric Desktop Shell**: A fixed 260px left-hand navigation pane accompanied by a responsive content canvas configured with a 12-column fluid grid.
- **Canvas Boundaries**: Main content canvases adopt a maximum container boundary of `1440px` centered on ultrawide monitors, maintaining eye-tracking continuity during lengthy grading runs.
- **Form & Input Rhythm**: Two-column layouts on desktop collapse cleanly into single-column flows on mobile/tablet viewports.

### Breakpoints & Adaptability
- **Desktop (`>= 1024px`)**: Full sidebar visible, 12 columns, 2rem (`32px`) margin, 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1023px`)**: Collapsed icon-only sidebar (72px), 8 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) margin, 1rem (`16px`) gutters.
- **Mobile (`< 768px`)**: Drawer overlay navigation, 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) margin, 0.75rem (`12px`) gutters. Stacked data cards replace wide table rows.

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Atmospheric Crispness
This design system rejects heavy dark shadows in favor of tinted ambient occlusion. Depth is established through subtle green-teal ambient shadows paired with ultra-crisp hairline borders.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Background surfaces (`#F7FAF9`) with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Content)**: 
  - `box-shadow: 0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(40, 122, 116, 0.06), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(40, 122, 116, 0.04)`
  - Border: `1px solid #E0ECE9`
  - Used for summary metric cards, table wrappers, and form panels.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Flyouts)**: 
  - `box-shadow: 0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(40, 122, 116, 0.10), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(40, 122, 116, 0.06)`
  - Border: `1px solid #CFE2DE`
  - Used for interactive card hovers, quick search popovers, and contextual dropdowns.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Drawers)**: 
  - `box-shadow: 0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(22, 51, 48, 0.16), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(22, 51, 48, 0.08)`
  - Backdrop: `rgba(22, 51, 48, 0.40)` with a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`
  - Used for student profile quick views, report card generation dialogs, and mutation confirmations.

## Shapes

### Geometry & Radius Rules
A uniform `roundedness: 2` (base `0.5rem` / `8px`) guides the interface:
- **Base Components (`8px`)**: Input fields, standard buttons, table cell action menus, and dropdown items.
- **Containers (`16px` / `rounded-lg`)**: Academic summary cards, modal sheets, schedule grids, and panel wrappers.
- **Hero Containers (`24px` / `rounded-xl`)**: Top dashboard welcome banners and announcement highlight cards.
- **Pills (`9999px`)**: Micro-status badges, Tahfidz juz counters, and circular avatar indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid Deep Pine Teal (`#287A74`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle elevation on hover with background transition to `#216661`. Height: 40px (Desktop), 44px (Mobile touch targets).
- **Secondary Button**: `#EFF7F5` background, `#287A74` text, `1px solid #CFE2DE`. Hover transition to `#E0ECE9`.
- **Accent Action**: Mint Foam (`#AEEED3`) fill with `#163330` text for primary confirmation steps in evaluation workflows.

### Micro-Badge Statuses
Compact status indicators featuring a pill shape (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical padding, and `space-sm` horizontal padding with `label-sm` typography:
- **Active / Lulus (Passed)**: Mint Foam (`#AEEED3`) background with `#1A5C55` text.
- **Review / Menunggu (Pending)**: Pale Cream (`#FFF8B0`) background with `#695E05` text.
- **Incomplete / Perbaikan**: Soft red tint (`#FDE8E8`) background with `#9B1C1C` text.
- **Tahfidz Track**: Pale teal tint (`#E0F2F0`) background with `#287A74` text and dot icon.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Background `#FFFFFF` with a `1px solid #E0ECE9` border and `8px` corner radius.
- **Focus State**: `border-color: #287A74`, accompanied by an outer halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(40, 122, 116, 0.15)`.
- Helper text rendered in `body-sm` with `#5C7A76`.

### Academic Data Tables
- Clean horizontal divider styling with `#FFFFFF` background.
- Header rows utilize `#EFF7F5`, uppercase `label-md` text (`#5C7A76`), and `12px` vertical padding.
- Alternating subtle hover row effect (`#F7FAF9`).
- Numeric columns (NISN, Scores, Class Rankings) right-aligned with monospace/tabular numerical formatting.

### Tahfidz Progress Card
- Specialized scholastic component featuring student name, target Juz/Surah, and a dual-tone progress track: base track in `#E0ECE9` filled by `#55A9A0`.
- Includes a quick-increment stamp button for mutaba'ah (daily memorization logging).

### Sidebar Navigation
- Deep-light balanced structure: Background `#FFFFFF` bordered on the right with `1px solid #E0ECE9`.
- Active menu item features a soft mint-teal indicator (`#EFF7F5` background, `#287A74` font weight `600`, and a left border strip of `3px solid #287A74`).
